Toniq works best on a bigger screen

This is a working prototype of a spatial events floor, built for a laptop. On a phone the room is too small to walk around in.

Open it on a desktop browser and you can move between zones, overhear conversations, and ask Toniq AI who to meet.

Answer Toniq's two questions on the right to unlock Join event.
Host tools are not part of this prototype. The attendee flow is.
Hosting your own event is coming soon. This prototype shows the attendee side.
